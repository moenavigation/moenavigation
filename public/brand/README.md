# MOE Navigation draft brand kit

These vectors extend the website's existing M-and-wave `BrandMark`; they are a draft identity for owner review, not a claim of trademark registration or legal clearance.

- `moe-mark-navy.svg`: standalone mark on a light background.
- `moe-mark-white.svg`: standalone mark on a dark background.
- `moe-lockup-navy.svg`: horizontal navy wordmark on a light background.
- `moe-lockup-white.svg`: horizontal white wordmark on a dark background.
- The matching application favicon is `src/app/icon.svg`.

Primary navy: `#082B50`. Supporting blue: `#345F8B`. White: `#FFFFFF`.

Keep the original proportions, use strong background contrast, and leave at least one-quarter of the mark's width clear around the artwork. The standalone mark is suitable when space is limited; the horizontal wordmark should remain large enough for NAVIGATION to be readable.

The SVG mark is fully path based. The horizontal lockups use editable Arial/Helvetica text; convert text to outlines in the final design application before sending artwork to a printer. Do not replace the neutral wordmark with invented Thai company/legal names. Obtain owner approval and appropriate identity checks before treating this draft as the official corporate logo.
